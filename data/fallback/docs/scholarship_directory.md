# Scholarship Portals Directory

## National Scholarship Portal (NSP)
- **Category:** SCHOLARSHIPS > Central Government Scholarships
- **Website:** [https://scholarships.gov.in/](https://scholarships.gov.in/)
- **Description:** Centralized portal for all national scholarships.
- **Govt Fee:** Free | **Service Charge:** Rs. 100
- **Required Docs:** Aadhaar, Bonafide certificate, Bank Account, Income certificate
- **Authorization:** None

## State Scholarship Portal (SSP Karnataka)
- **Category:** SCHOLARSHIPS > State Government Scholarships
- **Website:** [https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/](https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/)
- **Description:** Post/Pre-matric scholarships.
- **Govt Fee:** Free | **Service Charge:** Rs. 100
- **Required Docs:** SATS ID, Aadhaar, Income/Caste
- **Authorization:** None
